Optimization of Al₂O₃ ALD Process Conditions

Prepared by Gildong Hong · July 14, 2026 · Ver 1.0

1. Introduction

This document summarizes the optimization experiments carried out to establish standard conditions for the Al₂O₃ atomic layer deposition (ALD) process. Growth rate and uniformity were evaluated as functions of deposition temperature and precursor pulse time.

3. Results

Saturation behavior was confirmed at 250 °C with a growth rate of 1.1 Å per cycle, and thickness deviation across the full wafer was measured within 1.5%.

A stable ALD window was confirmed over the 200–300 °C deposition temperature range

Thickness reproducibility of ±0.8% achieved over 1,000 cycles
