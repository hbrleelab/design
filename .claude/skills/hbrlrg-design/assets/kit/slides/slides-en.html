02  ·  ALD FUNDAMENTALS

Four steps of the self-limiting cycle

Precursor pulse saturates at a single monolayer

First purge removes unreacted precursor and byproducts

Reactant pulse completes the composition by ligand exchange

Second purge closes the cycle. Thickness follows cycle count, not time
